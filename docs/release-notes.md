## peg2arm: Peggle 2 for Android (arm64)

**There is no Peggle 2 APK to download.** The app contains the game's own program, translated and compiled. So
everyone builds it on their own PC, from their own copy of the game, with the installer:

1. Download this repository (**Code > Download ZIP**, or `git clone`) and unzip it to a drive with 40 GB free.
2. Double-click `install\peg2arm-setup.bat` and follow the steps.

The full walkthrough is [docs/install-easy.md](https://github.com/aschaeffer9/peg2arm/blob/main/docs/install-easy.md).

**Attached:** `peggle_launcher.apk`, the optional cover launcher. It shows the Peggle games installed on your phone
(peg2arm, Peggle Enhanced, Peggle Blast) as box covers, and you add your own cover art. See
[launcher/README.md](https://github.com/aschaeffer9/peg2arm/blob/main/launcher/README.md).
